## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (For App Store FAQ Section)

**Q: Does PartMatch slow down my storefront?**  
A: No. PartMatch runs on Shopify Online Store 2.0 Theme App Extensions with lightweight assets and cached API endpoints. It will not hurt your Google Lighthouse or Core Web Vitals score.

**Q: Can I import fitment data from another app or spreadsheet?**  
A: Yes! PartMatch includes ready-to-use CSV templates, automated headers recognition (supporting exports from Easy YMM, Fitment Group, and Smart Search), and an automated ACES/PIES XML converter.

**Q: What are Universal Products?**  
A: Products like motor oil, cleaning fluids, tools, and general car covers fit every vehicle. In PartMatch, you can mark any item as Universal with 1 click so you don't need to map it thousands of times.

**Q: Does PartMatch support VIN lookup for all cars?**  
A: Yes, our VIN lookup connects to the United States NHTSA VIN decoding database, covering millions of passenger cars, trucks, and SUVs.
